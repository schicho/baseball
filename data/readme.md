# A database of every mound-charging we've found in Major League Baseball.

Copied from https://www.patreon.com/SecretBase/posts/database-of-weve-142735794


- Column A contains a newspapers.com link to an article of the mound-charging in question.

- Column B contains the date.

- Column C contains the name of the batter or charger. This is almost always the batter, but the definition of this particular individual is somewhat fluid. This person could be a player or coach who charges straight from the dugout or a baserunner. In extremely rare cases, it could even be an umpire or a common citizen.

- Column D contains the name of the pitcher. Like Column C, this category allows for some bend. I've decided that pitchers have the same right to incite a charging that batters do; therefore, a small number of pitchers you see here actually conducted a "plate-charging" of sorts.

- Columns E and F contain the teams that the chargers and pitchers from Columns C and D belong to, respectively.

- Column G is a grab bag of various notes I've scribbled down. These notes are incomplete and I can't yet vouch for the complete accuracy of every single one. Many contain links to video evidence I've found, or additional sources. In some cases, there's a lot more where these notes came from; I've squirreled away tons more research for some entries I'm already planning to expand into their own episodes.

Columns H and I contain the 0-through-5 ratings for the following categories:

- COLUMN H: PITCHER AND CHARGER

    Charger is almost always the batter, but can be anyone

    - 0 – Verbal

        Parties may take steps toward one another, but decline to physically engage

    - 1 – Fight averted

        Pitcher and charger are physically prevented from engaging, usually by an umpire or teammate

    - 2 – “Fight”

        Pitcher and charger physically engage, but there are no notable strikes or takedowns

    - 3 – Fight

        One or both parties score at least one clear strike or takedown

    - 4 – Strong performance

        One or both parties score at least one impressive strike or takedown

    - 5 – Clinic

        One or both parties score at least one exceptional strike or takedown, demonstrating extraordinary form and mastery of the art

- COLUMN I: GROUP PROJECT

    Primarily concerning teammates and coaches

    - 0 - Absent

        No teammates or coaches leave the benches or dugouts

    - 1 - Mill-about

        Players and coaches do spill onto the field, but no one engages physically

    - 2 - Fracas

        Physical engagement limited to pushing, shoving, or piling on

    - 3 - Rhubarb

        Clearly identifiable supplemental fighting

    - 4 - Melee

        Resembles a street fight. Typical qualities include multiple flare-ups, multiple simultaneous fights, breakouts into multiple clusters

    - 5 - DONNYBROOK

        Warrants police intervention. No longer exclusively a baseball incident

I've ruled that an incident must register a score of at least 2 in at least one of these two categories to merit inclusion to the database.
By my judgment, there are 264 mound-chargings between 1950 and 2025 that satisfy these criteria.
